Meet Marina

Marketing with intention.

I'm Marina Mikhael — a Marketing Strategist with a quiet conviction: that considered strategy, more than louder output, is what builds brands worth knowing.

My professional path combines marketing knowledge, practical business experience, and a strong understanding of customer behavior.

I worked as an Account Manager at a marketing company, managing projects and supporting businesses with their marketing needs. I also have experience in tourism and hospitality, which strengthened my understanding of customer journeys, service, and business communication.

Teaching Assistant – Business & Marketing
Taught and supported students in Business and Marketing-related subjects, combining academic knowledge with practical marketing experience.

Today, my approach begins with the business itself. I study the offer, the audience, the competitive landscape, and the goals before recommending a piece of content or marketing action.

The result is marketing with intention: clearer positioning, more confident communication, and practical steps a business can actually take — not just more posts, but a presence that supports real business goals.
