Common Questions

Frequently Asked

If you don't see your question here, just send a message — happy to chat.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Startups, small businesses, and solo founders across different industries — including service, lifestyle, education, and more. Most clients are early-stage or rebranding, where clear positioning and a steady content direction matter most.

How does the engagement usually start?

Begin with a free 20-minute WhatsApp or email conversation about your business and goals. If we're a fit, I'll suggest one of the services — most often Marketing Strategy or a 1:1 Consultation — as the first step.

Do you only work with Egyptian businesses?

Not at all. Many of my clients are based across the MENA region and beyond. All consultations and deliverables are conducted remotely, in English or Arabic.

What does a Marketing Strategy deliverable look like?

A clear written document covering business & audience analysis, competitor landscape, brand positioning, marketing objectives, and a practical action plan — ready to share with your team or implement directly.

How quickly can you start?

Most projects begin within 1–2 weeks of confirmation. If your timing is tight, mention it in your message and I'll let you know what's possible.

Prefer to talk it through?

If a written brief feels heavy, a WhatsApp voice note works just as well. Begin in whatever way feels most natural.
